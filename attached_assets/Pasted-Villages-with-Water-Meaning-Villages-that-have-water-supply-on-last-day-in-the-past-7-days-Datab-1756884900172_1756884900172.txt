LPCD (Liters Per Capita per Day) Section:
Above 55 LPCD
•	Meaning: Villages where water consumption per person exceeds 55 liters daily (good supply) for last day
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table →  lpcd_value_day7 ≥ 55
Below 55 LPCD
•	Meaning: Villages where water consumption per person is less than 55 liters daily (insufficient supply) for last day
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table →  lpcd_value_day7 < 55

Consistent Above 55

Consistent Below 55
