## 📝 Learning Log & Notes

### Lesson 1: The Big Picture 🍽️
- **Concepts Learned:** 
  - **Frontend (The Dining Room):** What the user sees and interacts with.
  - **Backend (The Kitchen):** Where calculations, logic, and order-processing happen.
  - **Database (The Pantry):** Where all data is safely stored long-term.
- **Real-world connection:** `ChlorineDashboard.tsx` is in the **Frontend**!

### Lesson 2: The Building Blocks of Frontend (HTML & CSS) 🧱🎨
- **Concepts Learned:**
  - **HTML:** The structural frame of the page (defined using "tags" like `<div>`, `<h1>`, or `<Card>`).
  - **CSS:** The styling, positioning, and paint (color, border-radius, font sizing).
  - **JavaScript:** The electricity/interactivity (changing pages, responding to clicks).
- **Real-world connection:** Spacing, layout, and colors in the dashboard cards are styling rules controlled by CSS.
