LPCD (Liters Per Capita per Day) Section:
Above 55 LPCD
•	Meaning: Villages where water consumption per person exceeds 55 liters daily (good supply) for last day
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table →  lpcd_value_day7 ≥ 55
Below 55 LPCD
•	Meaning: Villages where water consumption per person is less than 55 liters daily (insufficient supply) for last day
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table →  lpcd_value_day7 < 55

Consistent Above 55
•	Meaning: Villages consistently maintaining above 55 LPCD for all 7 days
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table → All lpcd_value_day1 to lpcd_value_day7 ≥ 55
Consistent Below 55
•	Meaning: Villages consistently below 55 LPCD for all 7 days
•	Database Fields: water_scheme_data table → All lpcd_value_day1 to lpcd_value_day7 < 55
Optimal Pressure
•	Meaning: ESRs (Elevated Service Reservoirs) maintaining adequate pressure for proper water distribution.
•	Database Field: pressure_value_7 in pressure_data table (latest day’s value).
•	Optimal Range: 0.2 – 0.7 bar.
________________________________________
🔹 Below Pressure
•	Meaning: ESRs with insufficient water pressure, potentially leading to poor supply at consumer endpoints.
•	Database Field: pressure_value_7 in pressure_data.
•	Logic: pressure_value_7 < 0.2 bar.
________________________________________
🔹 Above Pressure
•	Meaning: ESRs with excessive pressure, which can damage pipelines and fittings.
•	Database Field: pressure_value_7 in pressure_data.
•	Logic: pressure_value_7 > 0.7 bar.
