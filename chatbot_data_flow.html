The Four Main Steps Explained

1. You Ask a Question ?? ?? ??

You type or speak a question into the chat window. For example, "How many water tanks (ESRs) are fully completed?"

2. The Smart AI Brain Translates ?? ?? ??

The chat window itself doesn't immediately know the answer. Instead, it sends your question to our Smart AI Brain (powered by OpenAI). The AI acts like a highly trained assistant whose only job is to figure out what you want to know and where to look for it. It strips away the conversational fluff and tells the system: "The user is looking for a count of completed ESR tanks."

3. Fetching the Data ?? ?? ???

Now that the chat window knows exactly what to look for, it reaches into the Dashboard Database—the giant digital filing cabinet where all the real-time sensor data, region statistics, and scheme details are stored. It grabs the exact numbers needed to answer your question.

4. Creating Your Answer ?? ?? ??

Nobody likes reading raw numbers! The chat window takes the data from the database and magically transforms it into something easy to understand. It might build a colorful chart, a map, or just give you a simple, spoken answer. Finally, it displays this right on your screen.
